APP NAME (30 max)
Torchless: Dab Rig Control

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 max)
Heat your e-rig from your phone, shape each dab, and keep a journal of your jars.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Torchless runs your compatible e-rig from your phone over Bluetooth, and keeps a simple journal of what you dab.

HEAT IT
• Link your rig and tap Heat it. See the temperature, time left and battery at a glance.
• Pick a preset, add time mid-dab, or cut it early.
• The screen stays on while your rig is linked.

RIDE PLANNER
• Shape a dab as a temperature curve: tap a point, then + or −.
• Use a curve for one dab, or save it to a preset.

JARS
• Log your jars with up to 3 photos, a rating and notes.
• Pick what you're dabbing, and rate it after the session.
• The graveyard keeps finished jars.
• Browse the Global shelf. See what other people rate each jar and like their photos.

TORCH MODE
• Dabbing with glass and a torch? Use heat-up and cool-down timers you set yourself.

STYLE
• Choose how the home screen looks, plus the heat-up look and the rig's lights.

Accounts are optional. Your dab history and temperatures stay on your phone. Sign in with Google or Apple only if you want to share jar ratings or photos.

For adults 21+ where cannabis is legal. Torchless is not affiliated with the maker of your rig.
