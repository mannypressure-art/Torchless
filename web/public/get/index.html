Torchless

Heat-up control

Heat your Dr. Dabber Switch 2 from your phone and watch the temp live. For adults 21+.

On a computer?Scan this with your phone to set it up there.

  1. Get Bluefy

    Safari can't use Bluetooth. Bluefy is a free browser that can.

    Get Bluefy (free)

  2. Open Torchless in Bluefy

    Open in Bluefy

    Didn't open? Copy the link and paste it in Bluefy.

  3. Link your rig

    Turn it on, keep it close, tap LINK IT and pick it. Tap Allow if asked.

Next time, just open Bluefy. Torchless stays in it. If you close its tab, go to torchless.brickandsignal.com in Bluefy.

Keep Torchless open while you dab. If you leave, the rig finishes on its own timer.
